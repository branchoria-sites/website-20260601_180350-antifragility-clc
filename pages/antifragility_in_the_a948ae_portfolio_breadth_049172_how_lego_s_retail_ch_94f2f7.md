## Introduction

LEGO’s [portfolio breadth]({{ 'portfolio/' | relative_url }}) only becomes commercially useful if customers can actually encounter that variety. A catalogue containing hundreds of products does not create resilience on its own. The mechanism that turns variety into demand is distribution: LEGO’s network of branded stores, e-commerce platforms, retail partners and membership programmes gives different product lines multiple routes to different audiences. In antifragility terms, this matters because the company is not dependent on a single retail environment, a single customer journey or a single type of shopper. When one channel weakens, others can continue generating demand. When a new product category emerges, LEGO can often place it in front of the right audience without rebuilding its entire sales infrastructure. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

## Why More Products Require More Channels

A narrow toy portfolio can rely heavily on traditional toy shelves. A broad portfolio cannot.

LEGO’s current range spans preschool products, licensed entertainment sets, engineering-focused Technic models, adult display collections, seasonal gifts and home-decor-oriented products. These categories attract different customers, shop in different places and buy for different reasons. A parent looking for a birthday present behaves differently from an adult purchasing a display model for themselves or a shopper searching for a Mother’s Day gift. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

The company’s retail structure allows the same [brick system]({{ 'brick-system/' | relative_url }}) to appear in multiple commercial contexts:

* Traditional toy retailers serve children’s play products.
* LEGO-branded stores showcase the full portfolio and encourage discovery.
* LEGO.com supports specialist products and direct relationships with consumers.
* General retailers expose LEGO to customers who were not actively seeking construction toys.
* Membership and loyalty programmes help reconnect previous buyers with new categories. LEGO Store <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

This creates a form of demand diversification. A customer who first enters through a [Star Wars]({{ 'star-wars/' | relative_url }}) set may later discover Botanicals or Technic. A gift purchaser may become a repeat hobby customer. Variety becomes commercially useful because the retail system allows movement between categories rather than trapping customers in a single theme. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

## The Strategic Role of LEGO Stores

Branded stores perform a function that ordinary retail shelves cannot. They display the breadth of the portfolio in one place.

A supermarket or toy retailer has limited shelf space and must prioritise high-volume products. LEGO stores can present a much wider cross-section of the catalogue, including premium products, adult-focused ranges and exclusive items. This makes them a portfolio showcase rather than simply a sales outlet. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

The company has invested heavily in expanding this network. LEGO opened 147 branded stores during 2023, bringing the global total above 1,000 locations, and continued expanding afterwards. ## How Direct-to-Consumer Channels Expand Demand

LEGO.com solves a different problem. Physical retailers cannot stock everything.

As LEGO’s portfolio has expanded into hundreds of products, direct-to-consumer sales have become increasingly important because they remove shelf-space constraints. Niche products, premium collector models and specialised themes can remain commercially viable even if they are not suitable for every retailer. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

This matters because antifragility depends on experimentation. A company can test more ideas when distribution is flexible. Direct channels allow LEGO to support products aimed at smaller but profitable audiences without requiring universal retail adoption. A set does not need to become a mass-market blockbuster to justify its existence if it can find its audience online. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

The direct relationship also creates data advantages. LEGO can observe purchasing patterns across themes, age groups and regions, helping it understand which categories are attracting new consumers and which are deepening engagement among existing fans. That information feeds future portfolio decisions. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt1cdf90a38318ef56/the_LEGO_Group_Annual_Report_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the LEGO Group Annual Report 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreAnnual Report10 Mar 2025 — In 2024, the LEGO Group grew its consumer sales* by 12 percent during the year compared to four perc...</span></span></span>

## Retail Channels as a Portfolio Discovery Engine

One overlooked effect of retail variety is discovery.

A diversified portfolio only absorbs shocks if customers know the alternatives exist. Retail channels help move buyers across categories.

Consider a customer entering a LEGO store to purchase a gift for a child. They may encounter adult-oriented display sets, architecture products or Botanicals collections they had never previously considered. Likewise, an adult fan purchasing a collector model may notice products designed for gifting occasions or family building experiences. ## Why Retail Diversity Supports Antifragility

The key point is not that LEGO owns stores or operates a website. Many companies do both. The important mechanism is that the retail network is designed to match the diversity of the portfolio.

When toy-market conditions deteriorated in 2023, LEGO still grew consumer sales and [market share]({{ 'market-share/' | relative_url }}) while continuing to invest in retail expansion. The company repeatedly linked its performance to a strong and diverse portfolio, but that portfolio depended on equally diverse routes to market. A business selling through only one channel risks suffering when that channel weakens. LEGO’s combination of branded stores, direct e-commerce, retail partners and loyalty-driven customer relationships creates multiple pathways between products and consumers. That does not eliminate risk, but it makes the company less vulnerable to any single disruption.

Within LEGO’s broader antifragility story, retail channels are therefore not merely a sales function. They are the infrastructure that allows portfolio breadth to work as shock absorption. A wide range of products reaches a wide range of customers through a wide range of routes, making the overall system more adaptable than any individual theme, retailer or customer segment could be on its own.
