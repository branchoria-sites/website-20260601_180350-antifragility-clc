## Introduction

LEGO’s performance in 2023 and 2024 is one of the clearest pieces of evidence that the company can strengthen its position during industry stress rather than merely endure it. The global toy market weakened after the pandemic-era boom, with consumers becoming more cautious and retailers reducing inventories. Yet LEGO increased consumer sales, expanded market share and outperformed major competitors in both years. The significance for an antifragility analysis is not simply that LEGO remained profitable. It is that adverse market conditions appear to have widened the gap between LEGO and much of the rest of the industry, allowing the company to emerge with a larger share of a smaller market. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/news/2024/march/lego-delivered-topline-growth-and-outpaced-market-in-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">lego delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreLEGO delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 202312 Mar 2024 — The LEGO Group delivered topline growth and outpaced the...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/news/2024/march/lego-delivered-topline-growth-and-outpaced-market-in-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">lego delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreLEGO delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 202312 Mar 2024 — The LEGO Group delivered topline growth and outpaced the...</span></span></span>

## What the toy market was doing in 2023 and 2024

The backdrop to LEGO’s results was unusually difficult for toy manufacturers. After several years of pandemic-related demand, the industry entered a correction phase. According to LEGO’s 2023 reporting, the global toy market declined by 7% during the year. Circana’s global tracking data also showed a broader post-pandemic normalisation, with worldwide toy sales down around 2% in 2023. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/news/2024/march/lego-delivered-topline-growth-and-outpaced-market-in-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">lego delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreLEGO delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 202312 Mar 2024 — The LEGO Group delivered topline growth and outpaced the...</span></span></span>

The pressure continued into 2024. Circana reported that toy sales across twelve major global markets fell by 0.6%, while LEGO described the overall toy market as down about 1% for the year. The market was no longer collapsing as it had in 2023, but demand remained weak enough that many manufacturers struggled to return to growth. ## How LEGO's consumer sales outpaced the category

In 2023, LEGO reported consumer sales growth of 4% while the global toy market declined 7%. ## Why LEGO was positioned to capture share

Several mechanisms appear to explain why LEGO gained share while overall demand weakened.

### A broad portfolio reduced dependence on any single trend

LEGO entered the downturn with a product range spanning preschool children, traditional play themes, licensed franchises and adult collectors. Successful themes included long-running brands such as [Star Wars]({{ 'star-wars/' | relative_url }}) and Harry Potter, while newer partnerships such as Fortnite expanded the company's reach. Weakness in one segment could therefore be offset by strength elsewhere. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/lego-builds-market-share-as-ever-popular-toy-bricks-defy-demand-drop-31af4249" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wsj.com">[The Wall Street Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wsj.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Danish toy giant&#x27;s Harry Potter-themed sets and the Fortnite video game contributed significantly to its strong performance, particul...</span></span></span>

This mattered because downturns rarely affect all consumer groups equally. LEGO was able to draw demand from families, enthusiasts and adult hobbyists at the same time.

### LEGO benefited from the strongest category in toys

One of the most important structural advantages was category exposure. Circana's data shows that building sets were the best-performing major toy category in 2024, growing 14% globally while much of the industry remained flat or declining. Building sets had also been one of the strongest categories during the broader market slowdown. Circana <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.toyassociation.org/ta/PressRoom2/News/2025_News/circana-us-2024-toy-sales-remain-flat-moving-from-correction-to-consistency.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyassociation.org">[The Toy Association]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Toy Association Circana: U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2024 Toy Sales Remain Flat, Moving from &#x27;...30 Jan 2025 — Building sets grew for the fifth consecutive year and captured the top spot in...</span></span></span>

Because LEGO dominates the building-set segment, it was positioned inside the industry's strongest area rather than fighting against category trends. This does not fully explain its success, but it gave the company a favourable starting point.

### Adult consumers became increasingly important

Industry research increasingly highlights the growth of "kidult" consumers—teenagers and adults purchasing toys for themselves. LEGO has invested heavily in sophisticated sets aimed at builders over 18, including display models linked to entertainment, architecture, vehicles and popular culture. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/parents-bought-fewer-toys-except-lego-millennials-plastic-clutter-2024-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: businessinsider.com">[Business Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">businessinsider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">parents bought fewer toys except lego millennials plastic clutter 2024 3</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Key products included collaborations with &quot;Fortnite,&quot; &quot;Star Wars,&quot; and &quot;Harry Potter,&quot; with a growing focus on the adult market. ### Direct relationships with consumers strengthened demand

LEGO continued expanding its branded retail stores and digital channels while investing in programmes such as LEGO Insiders. In 2023 alone, the company opened 147 new branded stores globally. A larger direct-to-consumer presence gives LEGO more control over merchandising, customer data and brand presentation than companies that depend primarily on third-party retailers. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/lego-builds-market-share-as-ever-popular-toy-bricks-defy-demand-drop-31af4249" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wsj.com">[The Wall Street Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wsj.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Danish toy giant&#x27;s Harry Potter-themed sets and the Fortnite video game contributed significantly to its strong performance, particul...</span></span></span>

From an antifragility perspective, this matters because retailers often become more selective during downturns. Strong direct channels reduce dependence on those external decisions.

## What market-share gains do and do not prove

The market-share gains of 2023 and 2024 are strong evidence that LEGO's business model can benefit relative to competitors when industry conditions deteriorate. In both years, the company expanded consumer sales while the overall market contracted. That is consistent with a business becoming stronger under pressure rather than merely surviving it. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/news/2024/march/lego-delivered-topline-growth-and-outpaced-market-in-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">lego delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEGO StoreLEGO delivered topline growth and outpaced market in 202312 Mar 2024 — The LEGO Group delivered topline growth and outpaced the...</span></span></span>

Market-share growth therefore reflects both company-specific strengths and category-level advantages. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.circana.com/post/global-toy-sales-stabilize-in-2024-collectibles-fan-favorites-and-smart-spending-shape-the-market" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: circana.com">[Circana]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">circana.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Global Toy Sales Stabilize in 202430 Jan 2025 — Sales declined by 0.6% in 2024, versus 2023, according to Circana&#x27;s Retail Trackin...</span></span></span>
