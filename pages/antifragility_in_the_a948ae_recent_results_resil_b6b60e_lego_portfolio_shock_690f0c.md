## Introduction

LEGO’s strong performance in 2023 and 2024 was not simply the result of having popular products. A central resilience mechanism was the breadth of its portfolio. When parts of the toy market weakened, LEGO was not dependent on a single age group, franchise, price point or purchasing occasion. Instead, it could draw demand from children, teenagers, adult enthusiasts, gift buyers and licensed-franchise fans at the same time. ## The role of evergreen themes like City and Technic

A portfolio becomes a shock absorber when some products remain relevant regardless of changing entertainment trends. LEGO’s long-running in-house themes perform this role.

Themes such as City and Technic are not tied to a particular film release, television programme or short-lived cultural moment. City appeals to children interested in everyday vehicles, emergency services and construction, while Technic attracts builders seeking more advanced engineering-focused models. Because these themes are based on broad interests rather than external intellectual property, they provide a stable foundation of demand across economic cycles and changing media habits.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://toyworldmag.co.uk/the-lego-group-delivers-record-top-and-bottom-line-results-in-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyworldmag.co.uk">[Toy World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyworldmag.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Toy WorldThe Lego Group delivers record top- and bottom-line...12 Mar 2025 — The 2024 portfolio was the Lego Group&#x27;s largest ever, with...</span></span></span>

The company’s 2024 best-selling themes included both City and Technic, showing that internally owned product lines remained important even as LEGO expanded into many other categories. This matters because a portfolio built entirely around licences would be exposed to the success or failure of outside franchises. [Evergreen themes]({{ 'evergreen-themes/' | relative_url }}) reduce that dependency.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://toyworldmag.co.uk/the-lego-group-delivers-record-top-and-bottom-line-results-in-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyworldmag.co.uk">[Toy World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyworldmag.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Toy WorldThe Lego Group delivers record top- and bottom-line...12 Mar 2025 — The 2024 portfolio was the Lego Group&#x27;s largest ever, with...</span></span></span>

Another advantage is flexibility. City, Technic and similar ranges can be refreshed continuously with new vehicles, buildings and play scenarios without requiring a major [licensing]({{ 'licensing/' | relative_url }}) agreement or a blockbuster entertainment release. That gives LEGO a dependable base of products that can absorb fluctuations elsewhere in the portfolio.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/news/2025/march/lego-group-delivers-record-results-in-2024" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lego.com">[LEGO Store]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">lego group delivers record results in 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The company achieved record revenue and operating profit, outpacing the toy market with significant...Read more...</span></span></span>

## How licensed sets broaden demand beyond children

If evergreen themes provide stability, licensed themes provide reach. LEGO’s major entertainment partnerships allow the company to attract consumers who might not otherwise be active toy buyers.

[Star Wars]({{ 'star-wars/' | relative_url }}), Harry Potter and other licensed ranges connect LEGO products to audiences that already care about those fictional worlds. A Star Wars customer may purchase a set primarily because of attachment to the franchise rather than because they are looking for a traditional toy. That expands LEGO’s addressable market beyond children and parents.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://toyworldmag.co.uk/the-lego-group-delivers-record-top-and-bottom-line-results-in-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyworldmag.co.uk">[Toy World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyworldmag.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Toy WorldThe Lego Group delivers record top- and bottom-line...12 Mar 2025 — The 2024 portfolio was the Lego Group&#x27;s largest ever, with...</span></span></span>

The significance of this mechanism became especially clear during weaker toy-market conditions. While overall industry demand softened, LEGO’s best-selling themes combined both home-grown ranges and entertainment licences. This mix meant that demand could come from several directions at once rather than relying solely on children's play purchases.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://toyworldmag.co.uk/the-lego-group-delivers-record-top-and-bottom-line-results-in-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyworldmag.co.uk">[Toy World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyworldmag.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Toy WorldThe Lego Group delivers record top- and bottom-line...12 Mar 2025 — The 2024 portfolio was the Lego Group&#x27;s largest ever, with...</span></span></span>

Licensed products also create multiple buying occasions. A child may receive a Star Wars set as a toy, while an adult collector may buy a large display model based on the same franchise. The same intellectual property therefore supports different customer groups, price levels and retail channels. That diversification reduces exposure to weakness in any single segment.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/lego-sales-revenue-surge-decline-mattel-hasbro-toy-market" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: businessinsider.com">[Business Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">businessinsider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Business Insider Toy sales are on the slide — but not for Lego</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Business Insider Toy sales are on the slide — but not for Lego</span></span></span>

## Why adult-facing sets matter in downturns

The most important portfolio development of recent years has been LEGO’s expansion into products designed specifically for adults.

Traditional toy companies are heavily exposed to household spending on children. LEGO increasingly supplements that demand with purchases made by adults for themselves. Large display models, LEGO Icons sets, collector-oriented franchise products and the Botanical Collection have expanded the company's customer base beyond its historical core audience.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://toyworldmag.co.uk/the-lego-group-delivers-record-top-and-bottom-line-results-in-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyworldmag.co.uk">[Toy World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyworldmag.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Toy WorldThe Lego Group delivers record top- and bottom-line...12 Mar 2025 — The 2024 portfolio was the Lego Group&#x27;s largest ever, with...</span></span></span>

This matters because adults and children do not always behave the same way economically. Even when birth rates soften or parents become more cautious about toy spending, hobbyists, collectors and gift buyers may continue purchasing products that serve decorative, recreational or nostalgic purposes. The Botanical Collection provides a concrete example. LEGO reported that the range attracted new builders and became particularly popular for gifting occasions. A flower bouquet made from bricks competes not only with toys but also with home décor and gift purchases. That broadens the sources of demand available to the company.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://toyworldmag.co.uk/the-lego-group-delivers-record-top-and-bottom-line-results-in-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toyworldmag.co.uk">[Toy World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toyworldmag.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Toy WorldThe Lego Group delivers record top- and bottom-line...12 Mar 2025 — The 2024 portfolio was the Lego Group&#x27;s largest ever, with...</span></span></span>
